# Metro Study Guide

An interactive, offline-first study guide system structured as a metro network, designed for learners with ADHD and low reading stamina to master hard subjects through plain intuition before full exam depth.

## Navigation

**Guide**:
A self-contained, offline study experience for one course or subject, organized as a metro network.
_Avoid_: App, site, doc, textbook

**Line**:
A thematic group of **Stations** covering one major unit of a **Guide**, identified by its own accent color.
_Avoid_: Part, module, unit, track

**Station**:
A single focused topic stop on a **Line**, designed to teach one core idea visual-first before deepening into exam rigor.
_Avoid_: Chapter, lesson, section, page

**Beat**:
A single authored screen in Explainer mode holding one atomic visual, explanation, or interaction.
_Avoid_: Slide, screen, card, page

**Effort Cue**:
A short time-and-**Beat** estimate shown on the map and **Station** header so the learner sees how small a commitment each stop is before starting.
_Avoid_: Reading time, duration tag

**Checkpoint**:
An end-of-**Line** mastery stop that collects common exam traps and practice questions across the **Stations** on that **Line**.
_Avoid_: Quiz chapter, end test

**Review Line**:
The final line of a **Guide** where all **Lines** converge, holding the cheat sheet, flashcards, **Weak-Spot Queue**, practice exam, and source notes.
_Avoid_: Appendix, summary section

**Terminus**:
The target milestone at the end of the **Review Line**, such as an exam or final assessment.
_Avoid_: Finish, goal screen

## Pedagogy

**Plain Intuition**:
The first-contact layer of a concept—everyday language and interactive visuals with zero unexplained jargon, understandable by an 8-year-old.
_Avoid_: Summary, TL;DR, intro, simple mode

**Name It**:
The brief bridge right after **Plain Intuition** and visual exploration that attaches the official academic term to the everyday mental model the learner already grasped.
_Avoid_: Glossary intro, formal heading

**Exam Depth**:
The progressive rigorous layer of a concept containing formal terminology, exact formulas, proofs, edge cases, and every minor source detail.
_Avoid_: Advanced mode, extra notes, details

**Micro-Check**:
A lightweight, non-blocking active recall prompt placed inside a **Station** to engage the learner every 1–2 minutes between **Checkpoints**.
_Avoid_: Pop quiz, mini exam

**Tap-to-Predict**:
A one-tap **Micro-Check** asking the learner to guess what happens next before revealing an answer or visual step.
_Avoid_: Poll, teaser question

**Weak-Spot Queue**:
A local review bucket on the **Review Line** that gathers missed **Micro-Checks**, **Checkpoint** questions, and shaky flashcards for targeted replay.
_Avoid_: Error log, wrong answers list
