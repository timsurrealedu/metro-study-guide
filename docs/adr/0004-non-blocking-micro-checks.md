# Non-blocking in-station Micro-Checks

Status: accepted

Every **Station** includes at least one lightweight, non-blocking **Micro-Check** (**Tap-to-Predict** or a 2–4 item mini-drill) to break passive reading every 1–2 minutes, while full exam-style traps and 4–5 option quizzes remain in **Line Checkpoints** and the **Review Line**. We deliberately chose non-blocking prompts over gated progression (as in Duolingo or Brilliant) so tired or scanning ADHD learners never hit a frustrating wall; engagement relies on curiosity and instant feedback, while missed attempts feed the **Weak-Spot Queue**.
