# Two progressive depth layers (Plain Intuition and Exam Depth)

Status: accepted

Every concept separates first-contact **Plain Intuition** (zero unexplained jargon, short reading bites, visual-first) from collapsed inline **Exam Depth** drawers (formal definitions, formulas, proofs, edge cases, and every minor source detail), paired with a global "Show Exam Depth" toggle for exam-cramming passes. Writing one flat prose stream caused academic jargon to leak into opening explanations and overwhelm ADHD learners, while maintaining two separate full-guide rewrites would double authoring drift. Inline collapsed drawers preserve 100% exhaustive exam coverage right beside each idea without intimidating first contact; inside Explainer mode, opening **Exam Depth** expands inline with natural vertical scrolling at full 100% readable font size rather than zoom-shrinking or blocking modal sheets.
