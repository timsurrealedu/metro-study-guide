# Explicit authored Explainer Beats over character-count slicing

Status: accepted

Explainer mode steps through explicit authored **Beats** rather than slicing arbitrary Scroll DOM nodes at a 520-character heuristic. Character-based slicing broke thoughts mid-explanation, triggered jarring `part 1 of 2` pagination, and undermined visual-first pacing. Authoring explicit **Beats** adds light structural discipline in `content.html` (with graceful fallback for legacy guides) in exchange for predictable, bite-sized focus screens and accurate **Effort Cues**.
