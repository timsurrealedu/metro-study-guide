Connecting across an asymptote
Never draw a vertical line across x = 2 in \frac{1}{x-2} + 1. The function jumps from -\infty to +\infty and is undefined at x = 2.
Confusing slope with height
A curve can sit high above the x-axis while its tangent slope is negative or zero. Height reads f(x); tangent slope reads f'(x).
